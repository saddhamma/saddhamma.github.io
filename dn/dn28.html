\subsubsection{Sariputta's Lion's Roar}

1 Thus was heard by me. At one time the Auspicious One was dwelling in Nalanda at the Pavarika Mango Grove. Then the venerable Sāriputto visited with the Auspicious One. Upon arrival, having bowed down to the Auspicious One, he sat down to one side. Having sat down to one side, the venerable Sāriputto said this to the Auspicious One: “Thus, O Bhante, is my belief in the Auspicious One: Neither has there been, nor will there be, nor does there exist now, another samaṇa or brahmana who has higher supreme knowledge than the Auspicious One, namely, [with regard to] awakening.”

2 Lofty, O Sāriputta, is this bold utterance of yours. You have definately established a roaring of a lion's roar: “Thus, O Bhante, is my belief in the Auspicious One: Neither has there been, nor will there be, nor does there exist now, another samaṇa or brahmana who has higher supreme knowledge than the Auspicious One, namely, [with regard to] awakening.” How is this, O Sāriputta? Have you encompassed the minds of all those arahants, rightly self-awakened Buddhas of the past with your own mind so as to know that: “Those Auspicious Ones were of such virtue, those Auspicious Ones were of such dhamma, those Auspicious Ones were of such wisdom, those Auspicious Ones were of such dwelling, those Auspicious Ones were of such liberation?”

3 Certainly not, Bhante.

4 Then how is this, O Sāriputta? Have you encompassed the minds of all those arahants, rightly self-awakened Buddhas to come in the future with your own mind so as to know that: “Those Auspicious Ones will be of such virtue, those Auspicious Ones will be of such dhamma, those Auspicious Ones will be of such wisdom, those Auspicious Ones will be of such dwelling, those Auspicious Ones will be of such liberation?”

5 Certainly not, Bhante.

6 Then how is this, O Sāriputta? I am presently the arahant, rightly self-awakened Buddha. Have you encompassed my mind with your own mind so as to know that: “This Auspicious One is of such virtue, those Auspicious Ones is of such dhamma, those Auspicious Ones is of such wisdom, those Auspicious Ones is of such dwelling, those Auspicious Ones is of such liberation?”

7 Certainly not, Bhante.

8 So indeed here, O Sāriputta, you do not have knowledge of the minds of the arahants, rightly self-awakened Buddhas of the past, future or present. What then, O sāriputta, is this lofty, bold utterance of yours, having definately established a roaring of a lion's roar: “Thus, O Bhante, is my belief in the Auspicious One: Neither has there been, nor will there be, nor does there exist now, another samaṇa or brahmana who has higher supreme knowledge than the Auspicious One, namely, [with regard to] awakening.”

9 I, O Bhante, do not have knowledge of the minds of the arahants, rightly self-awakened Buddhas of the past, future or present. Nevertheless, the drift of the dhamma, O Bhante, is known by me. Suppose, O Bhante, that a king's border town would have a strong surrounding rampart with one door. There the gate keeper would be wise, competent, clever, one who keeps out those who are unknown, one who lets in those who are known. For him walking along the encircling path around the town on all sides, would not see even so much as a gap in the wall or a crack in the wall large enough for a cat to slip through. It will occur to him: “Whatever gross living beings are entering or leaving this town, all those enter or leave only by this gate.” So too, O Bhante, the drift of the dhamma is known by me. Whichever arahants, O Bhante, were rightly self-awakened Buddhas of the past, all those Auspicious Ones, having abandoned the five hindrances, mental defilements that are weakeners of wisdom, having enculturated the seven awakening factors with a mind well established in four establishments of mindfulness, they rightly awakened to the supreme awakening.

10 Whichever arahants will be rightly self-awakened Buddhas in the future, all those Auspicious Ones, having abandoned the five hindrances, mental defilements that are weakeners of wisdom, having enculturated the seven awakening factors with a mind well established in four establishments of mindfulness, they will rightly awaken to the supreme awakening. The Auspicious One too at present, O Bhante, the Arahant, the rightly self-awakened one, having abandoned the five hindrances, mental defilements that are weakeners of wisdom, having enculturated the seven awakening factors with a mind well established in four establishments of mindfulness, has rightly awakened to the supreme awakening.

11 Here I, O Bhante, visited with the Auspicious One for the sake of hearing the dhamma. For me, O Bhante, the Auspicious One taught the dhamma, [revieling] the highest of the highs, the most excellent of the excellent, the dark and the bright, along with their counterparts. I in just the way, O Bhante, as the Auspicious One taught me the dhamma, [revieling] the highest of the highs, the most excellent of the excellent, the dark and the bright, along with their counterparts, In just that way, from supremely knowing certain dhammas in that teaching, I acquired faith in the Teacher and arrived at a conclusion: Indeed, the Auspicious One is rightly self-awakened. The Dhamma is well-proclaimed by the Auspicious One. The Sangha of the Auspicious One's disciples has entered upon the good path. \subsubsection{kusaladhammadesanā}

33 punaca paraṃ bhante idhekacco samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā ātappavanvāya padhānamanvāya anuyogamanvāya appamādamanvāya sammāmanasikāramanvāya tathā rūpaṃ cetosamādhiṃ phusati yathā samāhite citte anekavihitaṃ pubbenivāsaṃ anussarati, seyyathīdaṃ\footnote{seyyathīdaṃ - machasaṃ} ekampi saṃvaṭṭavivaṭṭaṃ dve pi saṃvaṭṭavivaññāni tīni pi saṃvaṭṭavivaññāni cattārī pi saṃvaṭṭavivaññāni pañca pi saṃvaṭṭavivaññāni dasa pi saṃvaṭṭavivaññāni amutrāsiṃ evaṃnāmo evaṃgotto evaṃvaṇṇo evamāhāro evaṃsukhadukkhapaṭisaṃvedī evamāyupariyanto. so tato cuto amutra udapādiṃ, tatrāpāsiṃ evaṃnāmo evaṃgotto evaṃvaṇṇo evamāhāro evaṃsukhadukkhapaṭisaṃvedī evamāyupariyanto so tato cuto idhuppannoti. iti sākāraṃ sauddesaṃ anekavihitaṃ pubbenivāsaṃ anussarati. so evamāha: atītampāhaṃ addhānaṃ jānāmi saṃvaṭṭi vā loko vivaññī vā loko, anāgatañca khvāhaṃ addhānaṃ jānāmi saṃvaṭṭissati vā loko vivaṭṭissati vāti. sassato attā ca loko ca vañjho kuṭaṭṭho esikaṭṭhāyī ṭhito teva sattā sandhāvanti saṃsaranti cavanti upapajjanti. atthitveva sassatisamanti. ayaṃ bhante dutiyo sassatavādo.

37 aparaṃ pana bhante etadānuttariyaṃ yathā bhagavā dhammaṃ deseti iddhividhāsu. dvemā bhante iddhividhāyo\footnote{iddhiye - pts}. atthi bhante iddhi yā sāsavā saupadhikā no ariyāti vuccati. atthi bhante iddhi yā anāsavā anupadhikā ariyāti. katamā ca bhante iddhi yā sāsavā\footnote{iddhisāsavā - machasaṃ} saupadhikā no ariyāti vuccati? idha bhante ekacco samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā ātappamanvāya padhānamanvāya anuyogamanvāya appamādamanvāya sammāmanasikāramanvāya tathārūpaṃ cetosamādhiṃ phusati yathā samāhite citte anekavihitaṃ iddhividhaṃ paccanuhoti: eko pi hutvā bahudhā hoti, bahudhā pi hutvā eko hoti, āvibhāvaṃ tirobhavaṃ tirokuḍḍaṃ\footnote{tirokaṭṭaṃ - machasaṃ} tiropākāraṃ tiropabbataṃ asajjamāno\footnote{abhijjamāno - pts} gacchati seyyathāpi ākāse. paṭhaviyā pi ummujjanimujjaṃ karoti seyyathāpi udake, udake pi abhijjamāne gacchati seyyathāpi paṭhaviyaṃ, ākāse pi pallaṅkena kamati seyyathāpi pakkhi sakuṇo. ime pi candima suriye\footnote{mūriye - machasaṃ} evaṃ mahiddhike evaṃ mahānubhāve pāṇinā parimasati\footnote{parāmasati - sīmu} parimajjati. yāva brahmalokā pi kāyena vasaṃ vatteti. ayaṃ bhante iddhi yā sāsavā saupadhikā no ariyā ti vuccati.

40 sace maṃ bhante evaṃ puccheyya kinnu kho āvuso sāriputta, ahesuṃ atītamaddhānaṃ aññe samaṇo vā brāhmaṇā vā bhagavatā bhiyyo bhiññatarā sambodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante noti vadeyyaṃ. kiṃ panāvuso sāriputta bhavissanti anāgatamaddhānaṃ aññe samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā bhagavatā bhiyyobhiññatarā sabbodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante noti vadeyyaṃ. kiṃ panāvuso sāriputta atthetarahi añño samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā bhagavatā bhiyyobhiññataro sambodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante noti vadeyyaṃ. sace pana maṃ bhante evaṃ puccheyya kinnu kho āvuso sāriputta ahesuṃ atītamaddhānaṃ aññe samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā bhagavatā samasamā sambodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante evanti vadeyyaṃ. kiṃ panāvuso sāriputta, bhavissanti anāgatamaddhānaṃ aññe samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā bhagavatā samasamā sambodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante evanti vadeyyaṃ. kaṃ panāvuso sāriputta atthetarahi aññe samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā bhagavatā samasamā sambodhiyanti? evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhantenoti vadeyyaṃ. sace pana maṃ bhante evaṃ puccheyya, kiṃ panāyasmā sāriputto ekaccaṃ abbhanujānāti ekaccaṃ nābbhanujānātī?ti evaṃ puṭṭho ahaṃ bhante evaṃ byākareyyaṃ sammukhā metaṃ āvuso bhagavato sutaṃ, sammukhā paṭiggahitaṃ. ahesuṃ atītamaddhānaṃ arahanto sammāsambuddhā mayā samasamā sambodhiyanti. sammukhā metaṃ āvuso bhagavato sutaṃ, sammukhā paṭiggahitaṃ: bhavissanti anāgatamaddhānaṃ arahanto sammāsambuddhā mayā samasamā sambodhiyanti. sammukhā metaṃ āvuso bhagavato sutaṃ sammukhā paṭiggahitaṃ:

41 aṭṭhānametaṃ anavakāso yaṃ ekissā lokadhātuyā dve arahanto sammāsambuddhā apubbaṃ acarimaṃ uppajeyyuṃ. netaṃ ṭhānaṃ vijjatīti. ekaccāhaṃ bhante evaṃ puṭṭho evaṃ byākaramāno vuttavādī ceva bhagavato homi. na ca bhagavantaṃ abhutena abbhācikkhāmi, dhammassa cānudhammaṃ byākaromi, na ca koci sahadhammiko. vādānupāto\footnote{vādānuvāde - machasaṃ} gārayhaṃ ṭhānaṃ āgacchatī?ti.

42 tagagha tvaṃ sāriputta evaṃ puṭṭho evaṃ byākaramāno vuttavādi ceva me hoti na ca maṃ abhutena abbhācikkhasi, dhammassa cānudhammaṃ byākarosi, na ca koci sahadhammiko vādānupāto gārayhaṃ ṭhānaṃ āgacchatiti. \subsubsection{acchariyabbhūtāni}

43 evaṃ vutte āyasmā udāyī bhagavantaṃ etadavoca: acchariyaṃ bhante abbhūtaṃ bhante tathāgatassa apapicchatā santuṭṭhitā sallekhatā yatra hī nāma tathāgato evaṃmahiddhiko evaṃmahānubhāvo, atha ca pana nevattānaṃ pātukarissati. ekamekaṃ cepi ito bhante dhammaṃ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā attani samanupasseyyuṃ, te tāvatakeneva paṭākaṃ parihareyyuṃ. acchariyaṃ bhante abbhūtaṃ bhante tathāgatassa appicchatā santuṭṭhitā sallekhatā, yatra hi nāma tathāgato evaṃmahiddhiko evaṃmahānubhavo, atha ca pana nevattānaṃ pātukarissatīti.

44 passa kho tvaṃ udāyi: tathāgatassa appicchatā santuṭṭhitā sallekhatā, yatra hi nāma tathāgato evaṃ mahiddhiko evaṃmahānubhāvo, atha ca pana nevattānaṃ pātukarissati. ekamekaṃ cepi ito udāyi dhammaṃ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā attani samanupasseyyuṃ, te tāvatakeneva paṭākaṃ parihareyyuṃ. passa kho tvaṃ udāyi: tathāgatassa apapicchatā santuṭṭhitā sallekhatā, yatra hi nāma tathāgato evaṃmahiddhiko evaṃmahānubhāvo, atha ca pana nevattānaṃ pātukarissatīti.

45 atha kho bhagavā āyasmantaṃ, sāriputtaṃ āmantesi: tasmātiha\footnote{yasmā - syā} tvaṃ sāriputta imaṃ dhammapariyāyaṃ abhikkhaṇaṃ bhāseyyāsi bhikkhunaṃ bhikkhunīnaṃ upāsakānaṃ upāsikānaṃ. yesampi hi sāriputta moghapurisānaṃ bhavissati tathāgate kaṅkhā vā vimati vā tesamimaṃ\footnote{tesampi imaṃ - pts; tesampi maṃ - syā} dhammapariyāyaṃ sutvā yā tathāgate kaṅkhā vā vimati vā sā pahīyissatīti. itihidaṃ āyasmā sāriputto bhagavato sammukhā sampasādaṃ pavedesi. tasmā imassa veyyākaraṇassa sampasādaniyantveva adhivacananti.
