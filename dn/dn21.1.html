Digha Nikaya

DN21: Sakkapañha Sutta - Sakka's Questions

  • A Saddhamma Study Group translation from the Pāḷi text.
    Get PDFs: Side-by-side | Par-by-par | Facing pages | English only | Pali only.

1 abhijānāsi no tvaṃ devānaminda, ime pañhe aññe samaṇabrāhmaṇe pucchitā”ti.

2 “abhijānāmahaṃ bhante, ime pañhe aññe samaṇabrāhmaṇe pucchitā”ti.

3 “yathākathaṃ pana te devānaminda byākaṃsu, sace te agaru bhāsassū”ti.

4 “na kho me bhante, garu yatthassa bhagavā nisinno bhagavantarūpo vā”ti.

5 “tena hi devānaminda bhāsassū”ti.

6 “ye svāhaṃ1 bhante, maññāmi samaṇabrāhmaṇā āraññakā pantasenāsanā’ti, tyāhaṃ upasaṅkamitvā ime pañhe pucchāmi. te mayā puṭṭhā na sampāyanti, asampāyantā mamaṃ yeva paṭipucchanti’ ko nāmo āyasmā?’ti. tesāhaṃ puṭṭho byākaromi: “ahaṃ kho mārisa, sakko devānamindo’ti. te mamaṃ yeva uttariṃ paṭipucchanti ‘kiṃ panāyasmā devānaminda kammaṃ katvā imaṃ ṭhānaṃ patto’ti. tesāhaṃ yathāsutaṃ yathāpariyattaṃ dhammaṃ desemi. te tāvatakeneva attamanā honti ‘sakko ca no devānamindo diṭṭho yañca no apucchimha tañca no byākāsī’ti. te aññadatthu mamaṃ yeva sāvakā sampajjanti, na cāhaṃ tesaṃ. ahaṃ kho pana bhante, bhagavato sāvako sotāpanno avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo”ti.

7 “abhijānāsi no you ruler of the devas, from this former (or past) of such form knowledge acquisition they (or present part.: being or ing) somanassapaṭilābhati?”

8 “abhijānāmahaṃ Bhante, from this former (or past) of such form vedapaṭilābhaṃ they (or present part.: being or ing) somanassapaṭilābhati.”

9 “talk about as ā however (or nevertheless or yet or but or else or still or moreover or and now) you ruler of the devas, abhijānāsi from this former (or past) of such form vedapaṭilābhaṃ they (or present part.: being or ing) somanassapaṭilābhati?”

10 “formally (once upon a time) Bhante, devāsurasaṅgāmo samupabyūḷho came to be (or was). in (or on/at/within/herein/among) that (or him) nevertheless (or ever so) Bhante, saṅgāme devā they (aorist of) be victorious, asurā they (aorist of) parājiyati. of/for that (or him) of me Bhante, that saṅgāmaṃ having abhivijinati of/for we (must) vijitasaṅgati this was (or occurred): ‘which (or (he) who) only (or simply or really) and now divine ojā which (or (he) who) and asurā ojā you (all) (must) ubhayametti devā paribhuñjissantatī (“|”). so nevertheless (or ever so) me Bhante, vedapaṭilābho somanassapaṭilābho sadaṇḍāvacaro sasatthāvacaro na for (or having) enstranged (dejected, disenchanted, recoil) na for (or having) fading away na for (or having) cessation of na for (or having) upasama na with supreme knowlege na for (or having) sambodha na for (or having) nibbāna leads to. which (or he/she who) nevertheless (or ever so) me this Bhante, for the Auspicious One the dhamma having heard vedapaṭilābho somanassapaṭilābho, so adaṇḍāvacaro asatthāvacaro one for (or having) antanibbida for (or having) fading away for (or having) cessation of for (or having) upasama with supreme knowlege for (or having) sambodha for (or having) nibbāna saṃvattatī”(“|”).

11 “what is however (or nevertheless or yet or but or else or still or moreover or and now) you ruler of the devas, atthavasaṃ considering of such form vedapaṭilābhaṃ mental pleasure acquisition pavedesī?”(“|”).

12 “six kho I Bhante, atthavase considering of such form vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemī”(“|”).

“Remaining just here,
[the state of] deva has come to be for me;
and gained is renewed life for me,
You should know thus, dear Sir.”

13 Seeing this first benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

Having passed from the deva group,
having abandoned the non-human life;
unconfused, I will enter a womb,
where my mind delights.

14 Seeing this second benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

That I, with unconfused wisdom,
dwelling delighting in the Instructions;
will dwell by the know-how,
contemplative and mindful.

15 Seeing this third benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

And faring by the know-how,
if there will be supreme awakening;
I will dwell as one with final knowledge,
and just that will be the end.

16 Seeing this fourth benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

Having passed away from the human group,
having abandoned the human life;
I will again become a deva,
in the highest deva world [sudhāvāsa].

17 Seeing this fifth benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

Those that are more sublime than the devas,
the glorious Akaniṭṭha [realm];
that will be my abode,
my final state of being.

18 Seeing this sixth benefit, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

19 Seeing these six benefits, venerable sir, I declare such an acquisition of feeling, an acquisition of happiness.

1 abhijānāsi no tvaṃ devānaminda, ime pañhe aññe samaṇabrāhmaṇe pucchitā”ti.

2 “abhijānāmahaṃ bhante, ime pañhe aññe samaṇabrāhmaṇe pucchitā”ti.

3 “yathākathaṃ pana te devānaminda byākaṃsu, sace te agaru bhāsassū”ti.

4 “na kho me bhante, garu yatthassa bhagavā nisinno bhagavantarūpo vā”ti.

5 “tena hi devānaminda bhāsassū”ti.

6 “ye svāhaṃ1 bhante, maññāmi samaṇabrāhmaṇā āraññakā pantasenāsanā’ti, tyāhaṃ upasaṅkamitvā ime pañhe pucchāmi. te mayā puṭṭhā na sampāyanti, asampāyantā mamaṃ yeva paṭipucchanti’ ko nāmo āyasmā?’ti. tesāhaṃ puṭṭho byākaromi: “ahaṃ kho mārisa, sakko devānamindo’ti. te mamaṃ yeva uttariṃ paṭipucchanti ‘kiṃ panāyasmā devānaminda kammaṃ katvā imaṃ ṭhānaṃ patto’ti. tesāhaṃ yathāsutaṃ yathāpariyattaṃ dhammaṃ desemi. te tāvatakeneva attamanā honti ‘sakko ca no devānamindo diṭṭho yañca no apucchimha tañca no byākāsī’ti. te aññadatthu mamaṃ yeva sāvakā sampajjanti, na cāhaṃ tesaṃ. ahaṃ kho pana bhante, bhagavato sāvako sotāpanno avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo”ti.

7 “abhijānāsi no tvaṃ devānaminda, ito pubbe evarūpaṃ veda paṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhanti?”

8 “abhijānāmahaṃ bhante, ito pubbe evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhanti.”

9 “yathākathaṃ pana tvaṃ devānaminda, abhijānāsi ito pubbe evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhanti?”

10 “bhūtapubbaṃ bhante, devāsurasaṅgāmo samupabyūḷho ahosi. tasmiṃ kho pana bhante, saṅgāme devā jiniṃsu, asurā parājiyiṃsu. tassa mayhaṃ bhante, taṃ saṅgāmaṃ abhivijinitvā vijitasaṅgāmassa etadahosi: ‘yā ceva dāni dibbā ojā yā ca asurā ojā ubhayamettha devā paribhuñjissantatī ti. so kho pana me bhante, vedapaṭilābho somanassapaṭilābho sadaṇḍāvacaro sasatthāvacaro na nibbidāya na virāgāya na nirodhāya na upasamāya na abhiññāya na sambodhāya na nibbānāya saṃvattati. yo kho pana me ayaṃ bhante, bhagavato dhammaṃ sutvā vedapaṭilābho somanassapaṭilābho, so adaṇḍāvacaro asatthāvacaro ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattatī”ti.

11 “kiṃ pana tvaṃ devānaminda, atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassa paṭilābhaṃ pavedesī?”ti.

12 “cha kho ahaṃ bhante, atthavase sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemī”ti.

“idheva tiṭṭhamānassa,
devabhūtassa me sato;
punarāyu ca me laddho,
evaṃ jānāhi mārisā”ti.

13 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, paṭhamaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

cuto’haṃ diviyā kāyā,
āyuṃ hitvā amānusaṃ;
amūḷho gabbhamessāmi,
yattha me ramatī mano.

14 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, dutiyaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

svāhaṃ amūḷhapaññassa,
viharaṃ sāsane rato;
ñāyena viharissāmi,
sampajāno patissato.

15 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, tatiyaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

ñāyena me carato ca,
sambodhi ce bhavissati;
aññathā viharissāmi,
sveva manto bhavissati.

16 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, catutthaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

cuto’haṃ mānusā kāyā,
āyuṃ hitvāna mānusaṃ;
puna deva bhavissāmi,
devalokamhi uttamo.

17 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, pañcamaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

te paṇītatarā devā,
akaniṭṭhā yasassino;
antime vattamānamhi,
so nivāso bhavissati.

18 imaṃ kho ahaṃ bhante, chaṭṭhaṃ atthavasaṃ sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.

19 ime kho ahaṃ bhante, cha atthavase sampassamāno evarūpaṃ vedapaṭilābhaṃ somanassapaṭilābhaṃ pavedemi.
