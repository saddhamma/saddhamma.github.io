3: The Book of the Threes

39. Delicate

  • A Saddhamma Study Group translation from the Pāḷi text.
    Get PDFs: Side-by-side | Par-by-par | Facing pages | English only | Pali only.

1 I was delicate, O bhikkhus, supremely delicate, excessively delicate. Indeed at my father's residence, these lotus ponds were built purposely for me. In one entirely white lotuses were planted, in one red lotuses, in one blue lotuses. I, O bhikkhus, did not wear sandalwood that were not from Kasi. My turban, O bhikkhus, were from Kasi, my jackets were from Kasi, my inner robes were from Kasi, my outer robes were from Kasi. A white parasol, O bhikkhus, was held over me day and night [with the thought]: “Let neither cold, nor heat, nor grass, nor dust, nor dew touch him.” For me, O bhikkhus, there were three mansions: one for the cold season, one for the hot season, one for the rainy season. During the four months of the rainy season, O bhikkhus, I amused myself in the rainy season mansion with entirely female musicians without descending to the lower floors of the mansion. And just as, O bhikkhus, at other residences, servants, labourers and employees were offered meal of rice husk porridge accompanied with sour gruel, in exactly the same way, O bhikkhus, at my father's residence, servants, labourers and employees were offered meal of fine rice accompanied with meat.

2 Of me being endowed with such great fortune, O bhikkhus, with such delicacy, it occurred to me: ``For the Dhamma-deaf puthujjana, himself being liable to old age, not exempt, having seen others who are old, becomes troubled, humiliated, disgusted, having overlooked his own [liability]. But if I, being liable to old age, not exempt, having seen others who are old, were to become troubled, humiliated, disgusted, that would not be proper for me.'' Of me, O bhikkhus, reflecting thus, I abandoned all the youth's intoxication with youth.

2 tassa mayhaṃ bhikkhave evarūpāya iddhiyā samannāgatassa evarūpena ca sukhumālena etadahosi: ‘assutavā kho puthujjano attanā jarādhammo samāno jaraṃ anatīto paraṃ jiṇṇaṃ disvā aṭṭīyati, harāyati, jigucchati, attānaññeva atiyitvā.5 ahampi khomhi jarādhammo, jaraṃ anatīto. ahaṃ ceva kho pana jarādhammo samāno jaraṃ anatīto, paraṃ jiṇṇaṃ disvā aṭṭīyeyyaṃ, harāyeyyaṃ, jiguccheyyaṃ, na me taṃ assa patirūpanti’. tassa mayhaṃ bhikkhave iti paṭisañcikkhato yo yobbane yobbanamado, so sabbaso pahīyi.

3 assutavā kho puthujjano attanā vyādhidhammo samāno vyādhiṃ anatīto, paraṃ vyādhitaṃ disvā aṭṭīyati, harāyati, jigucchati, attānaññeva atiyitvā5, ahampi khomhi vyādhidhammo, vyādhiṃ anatīto, ahaṃ ceva kho pana vyādhidhammo samāno vyādhiṃ anatīto, paraṃ vyādhitaṃ disvā aṭṭīyeyyaṃ harāyeyyaṃ jiguccheyyaṃ, na me taṃ assa patirūpanti’. tassa mayhaṃ bhikkhave iti paṭisañcikkhato yo ārogye ārogyamado, so sabbaso pahīyi.

4 assutavā kho puthujjano attanā maraṇadhammo samāno maraṇaṃ anatīto paraṃ mataṃ disvā aṭṭīyati harāyati jigucchati, attānaññeva atiyitvā1 ahampi khomhi maraṇadhammo maraṇaṃ anatīto, ahaṃ ceva kho pana maraṇadhammo samāno maraṇaṃ anatīto paraṃ mataṃ disvā aṭṭīyeyyaṃ harāyeyyaṃ jiguccheyyaṃ, na metaṃ assa patirūpanti’. tassa mayhaṃ bhikkhave iti paṭisañcikkhato yo jīvite jīvitamado, so sabbaso pahīyīti.

5 tayo’me bhikkhave madā. katame tayo: yobbanamado, ārogyamado, jīvitamado.

6 yobbanamadamatto vā bhikkhave assutavā puthujjano kāyena duccaritaṃ carati, vācāya duccaritaṃ carati, manasā duccaritaṃ carati. so kāyena duccaritaṃ caritvā, vācāya duccaritaṃ caritvā, manasā duccaritaṃ caritvā kāyassa bhedā parammaraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapajjati.

6 ārogyamadamatto vā bhikkhave assutavā puthujjano kāyena duccaritaṃ carati, vācāya duccaritaṃ carati, manasā duccaritaṃ carati. so kāyena duccaritaṃ caritvā, vācāya duccaritaṃ caritvā, manasā duccaritaṃ caritvā kāyassa bhedā parammaraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapajjati.

8 jīvitamadamatto vā bhikkhave assutavā puthujjano kāyena duccaritaṃ carati, vācāya duccaritaṃ carati, manasā duccaritaṃ carati. so kāyena duccaritaṃ caritvā, vācāya duccaritaṃ caritvā, manasā duccaritaṃ caritvā kāyassa bhedā parammaraṇā apāyaṃ duggatiṃ vinipātaṃ nirayaṃ upapajjati. \paragraph{-70.} yobbanamadamatto vā bhikkhave bhikkhu sikkhaṃ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattati. ārogyamadamatto vā bhikkhave bhikkhu sikkhaṃ paccakāya hīnāyāvattati, jīvitamadamatto vā bhikkhave bhikkhu sikkhaṃ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattatīti.
