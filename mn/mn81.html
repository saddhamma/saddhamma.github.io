Majjhima Nikaya

MN81: Ghaṭikārasutta - Ghaṭīkāra the Potter

  • A Saddhamma Study Group translation from the Pāḷi text.
    Get PDFs: Side-by-side | Par-by-par | Facing pages | English only | Pali only.

1 Thus was heard by me. At one time the Auspicious One was wandering on tour among the kosalans with a large Sangha of bhikkhus. Then the Auspicious One, having gone aside from the path at a certain spot, displayed a smile. Then of the venerable Ananda, this occurred: “What is the cause? What is the reason for the Auspicious One's displaying of a smile? Tathagatas do not display a smile without a cause.” Then the venerable Ananda, having arranged his robe on one shoulder, having stretched out his hands in anjali towards the Auspicious One, said this to the Auspicious One: “What, O Bhante, is the cause? What is the reason for the Auspicious One's displaying of a smile? Tathagatas do not display a smile without a cause.”

2 “Once upon a time, O Ananda, at this spot was a market town of a village named Vehalinga. It was very successful and prosperous and had many people, crowded with humans. It was this market town of a village, Vehalinga, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One lived in dependence on. Infact it was here, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, had his monastery. Infact it was here, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, used to sit when advising the bhikkhu Sangha.”

3 Then the venerable Ananda having folded his outer robe in four said this to the Auspicious One: “Well now, O Bhante, let the Auspicious One be seated, so that this very spot of land will be used by two supremely awakened ones.” The Auspicious One sat down on the prepared seat. Having sat down, the Auspicious One addressed the venerable Ananda.

4 “Once upon a time, O Ananda, at this spot was a market town of a village named Vehalinga. It was very successful and prosperous and had many people, crowded with humans. It was this market town of a village, Vehalinga, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One lived in dependence on. Infact it was here, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, had his monastery. Infact it was here, O Ananda, that the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, used to sit when advising the bhikkhu Sangha.” In Vehalinga, O Ananda, the market town of a village, was the supporter, the chief supporter of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One named Ghatikara the potter. Ghatikara, the potter, O Ananda, had a friend named the brahmin youth Jotipala, his dear friend.

5 Then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” When thus was said, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?” Also for a second time, O Ananda, Ghaṭikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” Also for a second time, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?” Also for a third time, O Ananda, Ghaṭikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” Also for a third time, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?”

6 Well then good Jotipala, having taken bathing sponge, we will go to the river to bathe. “Yes Dear,”, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala responded to Ghatikara, the potter. So then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter and the brahmin youth Jotipala, having taken bathing sponge, went to the river to bathe. Then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, this monastery of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, is near by. Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” When thus was said, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?” Also for a second time, O Ananda, Ghaṭikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, this monastery of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, is near by. Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” Also for a second time, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?” Also for a third time, O Ananda, Ghaṭikara, the potter addressed the brahmin youth Jotipala: “Good Jotipala, this monastery of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, is near by. Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” Also for a third time, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?”

7 So then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter, having seized the brahmin youth Jotipala by the waist cloth, said this: “Good Jotipala, this monastery of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, is near by. Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” Then, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala, having disentangled his waist cloth, said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “Enough Good Ghatikara, why bother seeing that bald-headed counterfeit samana?”

8 So then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter, having seized the brahmin youth Jotipala by the hair of his bathed head, said this: “Good Jotipala, this monastery of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, is near by. Good Jotipala, let us stop by to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. For good it is indeed, to see the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” So then, O Ananda, for the brahmin youth Jotipala, this occurred: “It is marvellous indeed sir, it is unprecedented indeed sir, in as much as this Ghatikara, the potter, being of inferior birth, will think that my hair should be seized when we have bathed our heads. Surely this will not be an ordinary [visit], I think.” He said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “You even milk it to this extent, good Ghatikara?” “I even milk it to this extent, good Jotipala. For such good indeed to me, is the seeing of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One.” “Well then, good Ghatikara, release me. We will go and see him.”

9 So then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter and the brahmin youth Jotipala, visited with the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. Upon arrival, Ghatikara, the potter, having bowed down to the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, sat down to one side. However, the brahmin youth Jotipala, exchanged friendly greetings with the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. Having exchanged friendly greetings and cordial talk, he sat down to one side. Having sat down to one side, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter said this to the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One: “This brahmin youth Jotipala, O Bhante, is my friend, my dear friend. May the Auspicious One teach him the Dhamma.”

10 So then, O Ananda, the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One instructed, aroused, energized, gladdened Ghatikara, the potter and the brahmin youth Jotipala, with a Dhamma talk. Then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter and the brahmin youth Jotipala, having been instructed, aroused, energized, gladdened by the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, with a Dhamma talk, having delighted in, having rejoiced in the words of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, having risen up from their seat, having bowed down, having circumambulated the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, they departed.

11 Then, O Ananda, the brahmin youth Jotipala said this to Ghatikara, the potter: “You look here, good Ghatikara, but why don't you go forth from the home life into homelessness after hearing the Dhamma?” “Don't you know, good Jotipala, that I take care of my old and blind mother and father?” “Well then, good Ghatikara, I will go forth from the home life into homelessness.”

12 So then, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter and the brahmin youth Jotipala, approached the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. Having approached, having paid respects to the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, they sat down to one side. Having sat down to one side, O Ananda, Ghatikara, the potter, said this to the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One. “This brahmin youth Jotipala, O Bhante, is my friend, my dear friend. May the Auspicious One give him the going forth here.”The brahmin youth Jotipala, O Ananda, gained the going forth in the presence of the Auspicious One Kassapa, the Arahant, the Supremely Awakened One, he gained the higher ordination.

19 atha kho ānanda, kikī kāsirājā kassapaṃ bhagavantaṃ arahantaṃ sammāsambuddhaṃ etadavoca: ‘atthi nu te bhante, añño koci mayā upaṭṭhākataro’ti. atthi mahārāja, vehaliṅgaṃ nāma gāmanigamo. tattha ghaṭīkāro nāma kumbhakāro. so me upaṭṭhāko aggupaṭṭhāko. tuyhaṃ kho pana mahārāja: ‘na me kassapo bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho adhivāseti bārāṇasiyaṃ vassāvāsa’nti, atthi aññathattaṃ atthi domanassaṃ. tayidaṃ ghaṭīkāre kumbhakāre natthi ca na ca bhavissati. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gato, dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gato, saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gato. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro pāṇātipātā paṭivirato, adinnādānā paṭivirato, kāmesu micchācārā paṭivirato, musāvādā paṭivirato, surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā paṭivirato. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro buddhe aveccappasādena samannāgato, dhamme -pe- saṅghe aveccappasādena samannāgato, ariyakantehi sīlehi samannāgato. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro dukkhe nikkaṅkho, dukkhasamudaye nikkaṅkho, dukkhanirodhe nikkaṅkho, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya nikkaṅkho.

20 ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro ekabhattiko brahmacārī sīlavā kalyāṇadhammo. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro nikkhittamaṇisuvaṇṇo, apetajātarūparajato. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro pannamusalo1. na sahatthā paṭhaviṃ2 khaṇati. yaṃ hoti kūlapaluggaṃ vā yo hoti mūsikukkāro vā3 taṃ kājena4 āharitvā bhājanaṃ karitvā evamāha: ‘ettha yo icchati taṇḍulapaṭibhastāni5 vā muggapaṭibhastāni6 vā kalāya7 paṭibhastāni8 vā nikkhipitvā yaṃ icchati taṃ haratūti.

21 ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro andhe jiṇṇe mātāpitaro poseti. ghaṭīkāro kho mahārāja, kumbhakāro pañcannaṃ orambhāgiyānaṃ saññojanānaṃ parikkhayā opapātiko tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā.

22 ekami’dāhaṃ mahārāja, samayaṃ vehaliṅge gāmanigame viharāmi. atha khvāhaṃ mahārāja, pubbaṇhasamayaṃ nivāsetvā pattacīvaraṃ ādāya yena ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkamiṃ. upasaṅkamitvā ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa mātāpitaro etadavocaṃ: handa ko nu kho ayaṃ bhaggavo9 gatoti. nikkhanto kho te bhante, upaṭṭhāko, ato kumbhiyā odanaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjāti. atha khvāhaṃ mahārāja, kumbhiyā odanaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṃ10 atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkāro kumbhakāro yena mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā mātāpitaro etadavoca: ko kumbhiyā odanaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto’ti. kassapo tāta, bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho kumbhiyā odanaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto’ti. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa etadahosi: lābhā vata me, suladdhaṃ vata me, yassa me kassapo bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho evaṃ abhivissattho’ti.11 atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkāraṃ kumbhakāraṃ addhamāsaṃ12 pītisukhaṃ na vijahi13 sattāhaṃ mātāpitunnaṃ.

23 ekami’dāhaṃ mahārāja, samayaṃ tattheva vehaliṅge gāmanigame viharāmi. atha khvāhaṃ mahārāja, pubbanhasamayaṃ nivāsetvā pattacīvaraṃ ādāya yena ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkamiṃ, upasaṅkamitvā ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa mātāpitaro etadavocaṃ: handa ko nu kho ayaṃ bhaggavo9 gato’ti. nikkhanto kho te bhante, upaṭṭhāko, ato khaḷopiyā kummāsaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjāti. atha khvāhaṃ mahārāja, khaḷopiyā kummāsaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṃ. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkāro kumbhakāro yena mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā mātāpitaro etadavoca: ko khaḷopiyā kummāsaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto’ti. kassapo tāta, bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho khaḷopiyā kummāsaṃ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṃ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto’ti. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa etadahosi: lābhā vata me, suladdhaṃ vata me, yassa me kassapo bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho evaṃ abhivissattho’ti. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkāraṃ kumbhakāraṃ addhamāsaṃ pītisukhaṃ na vijahi,1 sattāhaṃ mātāpitunnaṃ.

24 ekami’dāhaṃ mahārāja, samayaṃ tattheva vehaliṅge gāmanigame viharāmi. tena kho pana samayena gandhakuṭi2 ovassati. atha khvāhaṃ mahārāja, bhikkhū āmantesiṃ: gacchatha bhikkhave, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa nivesane tiṇaṃ jānāthā’ti.3 evaṃ vutte mahārāja, bhikkhū maṃ etadavocuṃ: natthi kho bhante, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa nivesane tiṇaṃ, atthi ca khvāssa āvesanaṃ tiṇacchadana’nti. gacchatha bhikkhave ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa āvesanaṃ4 uttiṇaṃ karothā’ti. atha kho te mahārāja bhikkhū ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa āvesanaṃ uttiṇamakaṃsu. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa mātāpitaro bhikkhū etadavocuṃ: ke āvesanaṃ uttiṇaṃ karontī’ti, bhikkhū bhagini, kassapassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa gandhakuṭi2 ovassatī’ti. haratha bhante, bhadramukhāti. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkāro kumbhakāro yena mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā mātāpitaro etadavoca: ke āvesanaṃ uttiṇamakaṃsū’ti. bhikkhū tāta, kassapassa kira5 bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa gandhakuṭi2 ovassatī’ti. atha kho mahārāja, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa etadahosi: lābhā vata me, suladdhaṃ vata me, yassa me kassapo bhagavā arahaṃ sammāsambuddho evaṃ abhivissattho’ti. atha kho mahārāja ghaṭīkāraṃ kumbhakāraṃ addhamāsaṃ pītisukhaṃ na vijahi, sattāhaṃ mātāpitunnaṃ. atha kho taṃ mahārāja āvesanaṃ sabbantaṃ temāsaṃ ākāsacchadanaṃ aṭṭhāsi, na cātivassi6 evarūpo ca mahārāja, ghaṭīkāro kumbhakāroti.

25 lābhā bhante, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa, suladdhaṃ7, bhante, ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa yassa bhagavā evaṃ abhivissatthoti. atha kho ānanda, kikī kāsirājā ghaṭīkārassa kumbhakārassa pañcamattāni taṇḍulavāhasatāni pāhesi paṇḍumuṭikassa sālino tadupiyañceva sūpeyyaṃ. atha kho te ānanda, rājapurisā ghaṭīkāraṃ kumbhakāraṃ upasaṅkamitvā etadavocuṃ: imāni te bhante, pañcamattāni taṇḍulavāhasatāni kikinā kāsirājena pahitāni paṇḍumuṭikassa sālino tadupiyañca sūpeyyaṃ. tāni bhante, patigaṇhātū’ti8, rājā kho bahukicco bahukaraṇīyo. alaṃ me raññova hotūti.

26 siyā kho pana te ānanda, evamassa: añño nūna tena samayena jotipālo māṇavo ahosīti. na kho panetaṃ ānanda, evaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ. ahaṃ tena samayena jotipālo māṇavo ahosinti.

27 idamavoca bhagavā. attamano āyasmā ānando bhagavato bhāsitaṃ abhinandīti. ghaṭīkārasuttaṃ paṭhamaṃ.
